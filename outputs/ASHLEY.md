## Script

### Judgment calls

- **Funnel language kept.** P2 says "middle- and bottom-of-funnel content," because the funnel-stage ban isn't applied in this test and that is the intake's own description.
- **F5 adapted.** "Fully out of sales" became "fully out of production," and "runs without her" became "runs with her just filming." Whether she still takes sales calls is MISSING; the intake says she now only films.
- **Escalator wording.** I said "so many leads that she actually had to hire a full sales team to take the calls," matching the intake. The lead count is MISSING, so there's no number.
- **Business-model change left out.** "She is changing her business model" has no details in the intake, and adding it pushed the transition past 40%.
- **"super successful."** I wrote this for the intake's "very successful" to meet the word quota. It's a tone change, not a new fact.
- **P2 aside.** A4 ("leads don't equal revenue") would fit this pillar better than A2, but the rotation gave A2.
- **L15** drops "just like Ashley's" (her coaching business isn't B2B) and drops the case-study parenthetical (its truth is unknown).
