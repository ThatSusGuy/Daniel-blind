### Judgment calls

- **Name spelling.** I used "Jered" (the file name, which a person typed) rather than "Jared" (the auto-transcript). The spec also writes "Jared," but it's quoting the transcript. Either way it sounds the same on video. Confirm before shipping.
- **P1 label.** The bank's P1 OFFER label, "we rebuilt his offer," would be false: the intake's OFFER is stopping weak content, a studio, and an editor. I wrote the label from the intake: "stop the content that wasn't working and rebuild how it looked."
- **Before-views left out.** The intake gives ~500,000 a video in one place and "a few thousand views a month" in another, so neither is used.
- **F1 adapted.** "Brings in qualified calls with decision-makers every single week" became "he has a system that he runs completely on his own, without us." I added "films for a few hours a couple of times a month." Both facts are in the intake.
- **"Tens of millions of views a month"** (his current, self-run results) isn't used, because it has no exact figure.
- **Mirror adapted.** "B2B founders" became "business owners," "build" became "do," and "outside of referrals" became "for their content."
- **L15** drops "just like Jered's" (a plumbing business isn't B2B) and the case-study parenthetical (truth unknown).
