### Judgment calls

- **Name spelling.** I used "Chelcie" (the file name, which a person typed) over "Chelsea" (an auto-transcript, which tends to default to the common spelling). Either way it sounds the same on video. Confirm before shipping.
- **Total views left out.** The opening says 17.5 million and the body says "closer to 17 million." The spec's stats-board template has a slot for exactly this ("closer to [TOTAL]"), but the two figures conflict, so I left it out.
- **The 90% post-volume increase** is used in L7 only, as a stats-board number. The intake doesn't say which pillar caused it.
- **F5 adapted.** "Fully out of sales" became "fully out of production," and "runs without him" became "runs without her carrying it all on her own." The intake says the agency took over "most of" the production.
- **L2 detail.** L2 says her "content covers kids getting braces." The exact business type is MISSING, so I didn't say she runs an orthodontic practice.
- **P2 inference.** "hoping something would land" is my reading of "no clear strategy."
- **L15** drops "just like Chelcie's" (not a B2B company) and the case-study parenthetical (truth unknown).
