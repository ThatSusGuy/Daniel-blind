### Where the spec forced a beat

- **Four interventions, but the spec allows at most three pillars.** It also says "the number of pillars equals the number of interventions." I merged the two interventions the intake codes as OFFER (the studio setup and the interview-style content) into one OFFER pillar.
- **FM2 doesn't describe this client.** He had an office team; the problem was what he posted, not doing it all himself. FM2 is forced only by the DFY-P1 rule. Its 4B sentence would be false, so L3 and L4 use the intake.
- **The Stat Stack template's slots are business metrics** (revenue, clients, pipeline). None exist, so the hook uses views and followers. The hook fails QA's "2+ exact numbers in clients, revenue, or pipeline."
- **L4 is "Required" with a number, but the number is MISSING.**
- **The CTA condition** (C4) and **L15's "B2B companies"** come from fixed spec text and fallbacks.

### Judgment calls

- **Name.** I used "Daryoosh," from the file name, rather than anonymous mode ("an attorney"). The file name was typed by a person who knows the client, and the spec prefers featuring the founder. Confirm the name before shipping.
- **Practice area.** It's unreadable in the transcript ("our use license attorney"), so the script says only "an attorney."
- **Mirror adapted.** "B2B founders" became "business owners," "build" became "do," and "a system to get clients outside of referrals" became "a system for their content." The intake supports "no tracking or planning"; referrals aren't mentioned.
- **P1 before-contrast.** "every idea and every script had to come from him and his team" is inferred from the agency taking over ideation and scripting.
- **P2 label.** "the go-to attorney for people who actually need one" fills [BUYER] from the intake's stated reason (people watching a lawyer explain real situations are closer to hiring one).
- **F5 adapted.** I dropped "fully out of sales" (sales-call time is MISSING) and wrote "the whole system actually runs on just a few hours of filming a month." The intake says filming takes a few hours a month.
- **400 posts** go in L7, not P1, so the number isn't repeated. The intake ties them to six months, so L7 says "in just six months," separate from the hook's "a few months."
- **L15** drops "just like Daryoosh's" (not B2B) and the case-study parenthetical (truth unknown).
