[L2] When Adam came to us, he was an expert defense attorney with his own practice, but he wasn't yet an expert on social media. [L14 · F5 adapted] And now Adam is fully out of the content grind. He's back to running his practice, and all the system actually needs from him is one recording session a month. [L15] And we've generated hundreds of crores in sales pipeline for B2B companies, and we do this every single day. - SPEC WOULD STOP: missing who the script features. The client's approval (founder name, company, or anonymous) is MISSING. I wrote the script with the first name, Adam, because the spec says to "use the founder when you can."
- SPEC WOULD STOP: missing why the main intervention worked. The intake's only stated reason, that Adam trusted the agency and followed the plan exactly, isn't tied to any intervention. The hero pillar has no "why it works" line.
- Not a stop, but flagged: the timeframe is "a few months," not an exact figure. The field isn't MISSING, so I used it as given ("in just a few months").

### Where the spec forced a beat

- **FM2** is forced by the DFY-always-P1 rule. Its 4B sentence ("He was the only one doing sales. Every call, every proposal...") would be false, so L3 uses the intake's own failure (random posts, no strategy).
- **The CTA condition** "you're still doing all the sales calls yourself" is what C1 gives for FM2. It doesn't match a content story, but it's aimed at the viewer, so I kept it word for word.
- **L15** says "B2B companies" because CTA audience is MISSING.
- **The hook can't meet QA's "2+ exact numbers in clients, revenue, or pipeline."** The intake has none: every after-number is followers, views, or a ranking.

### Judgment calls

- **Flip wording.** The template's "expert at getting clients" became "expert on social media." Nothing in the intake says Adam was bad at getting clients; it says his social media had no strategy.
- **DFY label.** I removed "sales and" from "the entire sales and marketing process." The agency ran his content, not his sales.
- **OFFER positioning.** The [BUYER] slot is MISSING, so it's "the go-to defense attorney on social media," with no "for [BUYER]."
- **F5 closer.** "Fully out of sales" became "fully out of the content grind," and "runs without him" became "all the system actually needs from him is one recording session a month." The intake only supports the second version.
- **L5 Mirror.** "B2B founders" became "business owners"; "what they build" became "what they do"; "a system to get clients outside of referrals" became "a system for social media." Referrals aren't in the intake.
- **L15.** I dropped "just like Adam's," because a defense practice isn't a B2B company. I dropped the parenthetical about dozens of case studies, because I can't verify it's true.
- **L16.** I left out the optional "(just like we did for Adam,)." With C1 it would claim we took over his sales calls.
- **P1 before-contrast.** "Posting whatever he could, whenever he could find the time" is my reading of "busy running the practice."
- **Unused facts.** I didn't use "videos regularly reach hundreds of thousands to millions of views" anywhere, because it has no exact figure.
