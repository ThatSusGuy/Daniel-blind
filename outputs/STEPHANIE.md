- **"Posting every day"** in the hook combines "posted consistently for years" and "posted once a day."
- **"Consistently" in P1.** "consistently posting several times a day instead of once" uses the intake's non-exact "several." It's a statement about her new posting rhythm, not a number in L8.
- **Website visitors.** "Thousands of website visitors" has no exact figure, so P3 says "it's actually sending people to her website" with no number.
- **F5 adapted.** "Fully out of sales ... runs without her" became "Stephanie's content runs without taking any more of her time." The intake supports that (163% more posts, no extra time).
- **F2 not used.** F2 (Dominance) would match her superlative, but 4G gives F5 for FM2, and the superlative is already in the hook.
- **DFY label.** I removed "sales and" from the bank clause.
- **L15** drops "just like Stephanie's" (an e-commerce brand isn't B2B) and the case-study parenthetical (truth unknown).
