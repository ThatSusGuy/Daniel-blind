### Where the spec forced a beat

- **P1 is supposed to be FM3's antidote, TARGET, but David has no TARGET intervention.** P1 is the hero, which is the closest functional fix.
- **The credibility fallback** ("already successful in the tiny-home space") is used because Credibility is MISSING; the intake doesn't show it.
- **The CTA condition is about referrals**, which David never relied on. It's aimed at the viewer, so I kept it word for word.
- **The hook can't meet QA's "2+ exact numbers in clients, revenue, or pipeline."**

### Judgment calls

- **Before-numbers.**
  - Ad spend is stated two ways ("tens of thousands" and "thousands of dollars" a month), so the script gives no ad-spend figure.
  - For audience, I used "never passed 5,000 views" (from the body) and didn't also use "under 5,000 followers" (from the opening). The intake flags these as one fact stated two ways.
- **1.8 million followers** is not tied to the 90 days in the hook. The intake ties only the 50 million views to 90 days, and the hook's wording keeps them apart.
- **Mirror adapted.** "B2B founders" became "business owners," "build" became "sell," and "outside of referrals" became "without paying for ads."
- **F1 adapted.** "qualified calls with decision-makers every single week" became "over 1,000 leads a month without a dollar of ad spend," both from the intake. The 4G alternative, F4 ("hire more closers"), would be false.
- **F7 not used.** The iteration proof fits David exactly, but 4G maps FM3 to F1 or F4, so the iteration fact sits in P3 instead.
- **P3 wording.** "that's a big part of how he got to 20 million views a month" softens the cause. The intake doesn't say the remakes alone produced it.
- **L15** drops "just like David's" (a tiny-home seller isn't B2B) and the case-study parenthetical (truth unknown).
