### Where the spec forced a beat

- **The pillar-order rules conflict.**
  - The rules say: SWITCH goes in P1 or P2; P1 is the antidote (OFFER); and the hero (needs the "why") is one of the first two pillars.
  - Three pillars can't all fit in two slots. I kept the antidote and hero rules and put SWITCH in P3.
  - Consequence: SWITCH's bank body calls for asides A2 and A1, but asides never go in Pillar 3, so P3 has none.
- **The L9 transition came up as option 7.** It mentions "crores in pipeline" in a script for a client who bills in dollars, and it puts "you" outside the places the voice rules allow. The rotation landed on it and the spec says "word for word," so I kept it unchanged.
- **The ROI template has no "our client,"** which conflicts with L1's frame rule ("then 'our client, [NAME]'"). I followed the 4A template.
- **No time qualifier** in L1, because the timeframe is MISSING.
- **The hook's numbers** are a return and ad spend, not clients, revenue, or pipeline, so it fails that QA item.

### Judgment calls

- **$377.** The intake gives "about $377," from the transcript's "$370 seven dollars." I wrote "$377," following the intake's reading. Confirm before shipping.
- **The $50,000-a-day ad spend** conflicts with $10K a month, so it isn't used.
- **No ad platform named.** The intake doesn't name one, so P3 says "ads," not the bank's "Meta ads."
- **P1 label.** The bank's P1 OFFER form, "we rebuilt his offer," is false; the intake's OFFER is positioning (a studio, "wellness authority," local patients). I used the bank's positioning clause in P1 instead.
- **SWITCH label.** Rotation gave option 1 ("we stopped the cold outreach that wasn't working"), but Greg never did outreach, so I used option 2 ("a complete 180 on how he got clients").
- **"Consistently" in L7.** "he's consistently bringing in an extra $10K a month" rests on the intake's "first time he can predict his revenue." The intake doesn't say how long the $10K has held.
- **"That's what's now bringing in the extra $10K a month"** (end of P3) credits the ads. The intake gives the $10K alongside the ad spend behind it, though the spend figure itself is unresolved.
- **L15** drops "just like Greg's" (a clinic isn't B2B) and the case-study parenthetical (truth unknown).
