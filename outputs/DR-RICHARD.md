# DR-RICHARD: case study script

## Script

_Beat tags in [brackets] mark the spec's lines and are not spoken. Word counts exclude them._

[L1] Here's how we took our client, Dr. Richard, from under 2,000 views a video to over 100,000 new followers and 21.6 million views in just six months. [L2] So Dr. Richard runs a health and wellness business, and he was already very successful in that space. [L3] But when Dr. Richard started working with us, he'd been posting several times a day for years, all by himself, with almost no traction. [L4] He'd put out 869 posts, and he was averaging under 2,000 views a video. [L5 · pain math] And at that pace, it would have taken him over 20 years to hit 100,000 followers. [L6] But just 17 videos in with our team, one of his videos actually hit 5.1 million views, and his account just took off from there. [L7] Now he's gained over 100,000 followers and 21.6 million views. [L8] And he's consistently posting just once or twice a day.

[L9] So here's the exact strategy that blew him up in the first place. [P1 · fewer, deliberate posts (no 4C code) · hero · A5] So the first thing we did was cut his posting way down, and this is the piece that most people miss. [P2 · production (no 4C code)] Next, we really improved his production from A to Z: the pre-production, the post-production, how he looks on camera, and we trained his team in editing, so it's not all on him anymore. [P3 · SYSTEM] And lastly, we systematized the entire thing. Once that one video hit 5.1 million views, we built follow-up posts on that exact format and kept repeating it, because a proven format makes the next posts easy to plan. So now every post he puts out is built on something we already know works.

[L14 · F1 adapted] Now, Dr. Richard has a system that actually fills his DMs with people asking to work with him. [L15] And we've generated hundreds of crores in sales pipeline for B2B companies, and we do this every single day. [L16 · C1] And if your team is sending hundreds of cold emails and getting nothing back, just click the link down below, see if you qualify.

### Rules applied, beat by beat

- **Failure mode: FM4 (cold-outbound grind), as the nearest analog.** It's the only 4B mode about high effort with almost no response: "sending [N] cold emails a month and getting almost no replies," against his several posts a day for years with almost no traction. 4G (cold-outbound grind): F1, CTA C1.
- **L1 hook: From-To** (4A). There's no goal or ROI, and there is a clear numeric "from" (under 2,000 views a video). I considered the Before-Contrast alternate, since he posted "for years," but the most striking before-fact is the numbers, not the duration. 27 words, ending on "in just six months."
- **L2: kept.** The hook doesn't say what he does. It uses "So [NAME] runs a [COMPANY TYPE]" plus the intake's credibility ("already very successful" in health and wellness). It ends positive, so L3 may open with "But."
- **Setup: two problems in one sentence** (posting several times a day for years with almost no traction, and doing it all himself). The first is paid off by P1 and P3, the second by P2 (the agency trained his team).
- **L3 opener:** "When [NAME] started working with us," (rotation option 2), with "But" in front.
- **L4:** "869 posts" and "under 2,000 views a video," with the minimizer "under."
- **L5: pain math** (preferred, per the build procedure, step 4).
- **L6: breakout** (his 17th video with the agency hit 5.1 million views), ending on "took off from there." Its "But" flips the negative L5.
- **L7:** opens with "Now," not "But now," because a second "But" pointing the same way as L6 is banned. Two numbers.
- **L8:** "consistently posting just once or twice a day" (an ongoing rate from the intake).

### Where the spec forced a beat

- **P1 is supposed to be FM4's antidote, SWITCH, which he doesn't have.** P1 is the hero instead.
- **The escalator is skipped, though the spec says Daniel used one here.** The spec says Daniel's Dr. Richard script had an escalator ("DMs were flooded"), but the intake says Overflow is MISSING: "nothing shows demand outgrew the business." The intake decides, so there's no escalator.
- **Pain math couldn't be computed.** The spec's formula needs a current follower count and a yearly gain, and neither is in the intake. I used the intake's own projection ("20+ years to reach 100,000 followers") as "over 20 years." The intake labels it "agency projection, not a measured figure."
- **100,000 appears three times** (hook, pain math, L7). This is the exception the spec names for this exact client.
- **The CTA's cold-email wording** is fixed C1 text aimed at the viewer.
- **The hook can't meet QA's "2+ exact numbers in clients, revenue, or pipeline."**

### Judgment calls

- **L6 adapted.** The template's "after just [N] weeks ... closed a [DEAL SIZE] deal and [his] pipeline" became "just 17 videos in ... one of his videos actually hit 5.1 million views, and his account just took off." The intake measures the breakout in videos, not weeks or deals.
- **L7 kept short.** The DMs ("full of prospects," no count) went to the closer, not L7. There's no number for them, and putting them in L7 also pushed the transition past 40%.
- **F1 adapted.** "Qualified calls with decision-makers every single week" became "fills his DMs with people asking to work with him," which is in the intake.
- **P1 inferences.** "hoping something would stick" and "each one has a real reason to exist" are my readings of "posted several times a day for years with almost no traction" and "fewer, more deliberate posts."
- **L15** drops "just like Dr. Richard's" (not B2B) and the case-study parenthetical (truth unknown).
